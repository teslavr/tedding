Tedding: baby routine tracker

A calm diary for your baby’s first years: sleep, feeds, diapers, solids, temperature and growth on a weekly calendar. Keep it together with a partner or nanny. For iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: ceofarming@gmail.com. If something does not work, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what needs Tedding Plus?
The calendar, solids, temperature, height and weight, family sharing, the simple weekly numbers and the PDF for the doctor are free. Tedding Plus adds the weekly review and the sleep forecast. For the first 7 days after you first open the app, both are open for free. The yearly plan comes with a free week for eligible new subscribers; a monthly plan is also available. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe. Tedding Plus supports Apple Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Tedding. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period so it does not renew. Apple handles all payments and refunds; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but the app asks me to subscribe again.
On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I share the diary with a partner or nanny?
Open the tab with your baby’s name, then Family → Invite, and send the link, for example in Messages. The other person opens it on an iPhone with Tedding installed and signed in to iCloud. After that you both see and add the same entries. The person who invites needs some free space in iCloud.

Entries do not appear on the other phone.
Check the internet connection, that both phones are signed in to iCloud and that the owner’s iCloud is not full. Entries are saved on the phone in the meantime and sync by themselves once the problem is gone. The Family screen shows when sync is not working.

Will I keep my diary on a new phone?
Yes, if iCloud was on: sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and install Tedding. The diary loads from iCloud.

How is the sleep forecast calculated?
From your baby’s own wake windows and sleeps over the past week at the same time of day. Until there are a few entries, the app uses the typical wake window for the baby’s age and says so. The forecast is always a range, not an exact minute.

How do I make a summary for the doctor?
Open Report → PDF for the doctor, choose 7, 14 or 30 days and tap Send. The PDF has sleep, feeds, diapers, temperature, height and weight, solids and your notes. It is made on your iPhone and goes only where you send it.

Can I use °F, pounds and ounces?
Yes. The app picks units by your App Store country; you can change them in Settings on the tab with your baby’s name. Your choice always wins.

Is this medical advice?
No. The app shows what you recorded next to typical ranges for age. It does not diagnose. If something worries you, talk to a doctor.

Which devices and languages are supported?
iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The app is available in English, Russian, German, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil).
