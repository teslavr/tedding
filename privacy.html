Privacy Policy

Tedding: baby routine tracker for iPhone (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Summary

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not run a server. What you record about your baby is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud under your Apple Account. We cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

What you record

The baby’s name, date of birth and sex; sleep, feeds (breast side, bottle volume), diapers, temperature, weight, height and notes; the solid foods you try, how much was eaten, how the baby liked them, any reactions you note and which foods you mark as allergens.

App settings, such as units, night mode and which baby is selected, are kept only on the device.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so the diary syncs between your devices and moves with you to a new phone. That copy belongs to your Apple Account, and we have no access to it. Apple stores and protects it under its iCloud terms and the Apple Privacy Policy, and it may be kept in other countries, including the United States. Without iCloud the diary stays only on that iPhone.

For the widgets, the app keeps a short summary on the iPhone: the baby's name and the time of the last sleep, feed and diaper. A Lock Screen widget and the timer of a running sleep can be seen without unlocking; whether to add the widget and show the timer (Settings → Tedding → Live Activities) is up to you.

Sharing with family

When you invite someone on the Family screen, iCloud sharing gives them access to that baby’s whole diary: they can see, add and change entries. The shared diary stays in the owner’s iCloud. Apple shows the people in a share each other’s names or the contact details used for the invitation. The owner can remove a person or stop sharing at any time under Family → Manage access.

Calculations

The weekly review, the sleep forecast, typical ranges for age and growth charts are all calculated on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere for them.

The PDF for the doctor is also made on your iPhone. It goes only where you send it yourself, for example by email or in a messenger, and it includes the notes you wrote.

Purchases

Tedding Plus is a subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive or see your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account.

To suggest °C or °F, the app reads the country of your App Store account on the device. This is not sent anywhere.

Crash reports

If you have agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and general usage statistics. They do not include your diary. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Health information

Entries such as temperature, weight or notes can be about your child’s health. They are used only to show them to you and to the people you invite. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Typical ranges for age are for reference; if something worries you, talk to a doctor.

Children

The app is for parents and caregivers and is not directed at children. Information about a child is entered by their parent or caregiver, who decides what to record and whom to share it with.

Your choices

  • You can change or delete any entry. Changes and deletions also reach iCloud and the people you share the diary with.
  • To delete a baby’s whole diary, open the tab with the baby’s name and tap Delete diary. It is removed from that iPhone, from iCloud and for everyone you shared it with, and cannot be restored. If someone invited you, the same place says Leave this diary: it is removed only from your iPhone, and the owner keeps it.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPhone. The copy in iCloud stays until you delete it in the iCloud storage settings on your iPhone (Settings → your name → iCloud).
  • To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Tedding in the same iCloud settings.

You can write to us with any privacy question or request, and we will reply within 30 days. Because we do not hold your diary, we cannot view, export or delete it for you, but we will help you do it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
